# Data Dictionary — Customer Retention & RFM Dataset

## Overview
This document defines the verified schema, data types, upstream lineage, and business rules for `customer_segments.csv`, derived from the **UCI Online Retail** dataset (`Online Retail.xlsx`) and consumed by the Tableau **Executive Retention Dashboard**.

* **Grain:** One row per unique customer (`customer_id`) — **4,371 total customer records** (`0` nulls).
* **Reference / Snapshot Date:** `2011-12-10` (used to calculate `recency_days`).
* **Currency:** British Pound Sterling (`£` / GBP).
* **Dormancy Threshold:** `90` days since last purchase (`recency_days > 90`).

---

## Verified Schema (`customer_segments.csv`)

| Column Name | Data Type | Nullable | Source Column(s) (`Online Retail.xlsx`)[cite: 6] | Description | Verified Business Logic & Calculation Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `customer_id` | `FLOAT` / `INTEGER` | No | `CustomerID`[cite: 6] | Unique customer account identifier (`12346` to `18287`). | Distinct non-null `CustomerID`[cite: 6] retained after removing anonymous/unauthenticated rows (`CustomerID IS NULL`). Stored as float (`14646.0`) in CSV export. |
| `last_purchase_date` | `TIMESTAMP` (ISO 8601) | No | `InvoiceDate`[cite: 6] | UTC timestamp of the customer's most recent recorded transaction. | Maximum (`MAX(InvoiceDate)`) transaction timestamp per customer (`2010-12-01` through `2011-12-09`). |
| `recency_days` | `INTEGER` | No | `InvoiceDate`[cite: 6] | Number of days elapsed between the customer's last transaction and `2011-12-10`. | Integer day difference (`DATEDIFF('day', last_purchase_date, '2011-12-10')`); ranges from `1` to `374` days. |
| `frequency_count` | `INTEGER` | No | `InvoiceNo`[cite: 6] | Total number of distinct invoices recorded for the customer. | Count of unique invoice numbers (`COUNT(DISTINCT InvoiceNo)`) per customer; ranges from `1` to `210`. |
| `monetary_value` | `DECIMAL(12,2)` | No | `Quantity`, `UnitPrice`[cite: 6] | Cumulative net spend (`£`) generated by the customer. | Sum of net line-item revenue (`SUM(Quantity * UnitPrice)`) in GBP (`£`); ranges from `£0.00` to `£280,206.02`. |
| `is_churned_risk` | `INTEGER` (Binary) | No | Derived (`recency_days`) | Binary dormancy flag (`1` = Dormant / Churn Risk, `0` = Active). | Evaluates to `1` when `recency_days > 90` (1,454 accounts), and `0` when `recency_days <= 90` (2,917 accounts). |
| `monetary_decile` | `INTEGER` (`1`–`10`) | No | Derived (`monetary_value`) | Customer spend tier ranked into 10 equal-sized deciles (`1` = Top 10%). | Computed via `NTILE(10) OVER (ORDER BY monetary_value DESC)`; Decile `1` contains the top 438 accounts (`>= £3,640.67`). |
| `customer_segment` | `STRING` | No | Derived (`monetary_decile`, `is_churned_risk`) | Final behavioral retention tier used for dashboard filtering and CRM targeting. | Evaluated in strict precedence order:<br>1. **`Top 10% VIP`** (`438` accounts): `monetary_decile = 1` (takes priority regardless of recency; includes 23 VIPs with `recency_days > 90`).<br>2. **`At-Risk / Dormant`** (`1,431` accounts): `monetary_decile > 1` AND `is_churned_risk = 1` (`recency_days >= 92`).<br>3. **`Active Regular`** (`2,502` accounts): `monetary_decile > 1` AND `is_churned_risk = 0` (`recency_days <= 90`). |

## Upstream Source Lineage & Audit Notes (`Online Retail.xlsx`)
* **Raw Input Schema:** `InvoiceNo`, `StockCode`, `Description`, `Quantity`, `InvoiceDate`, `UnitPrice`, `CustomerID`, `Country`[cite: 6].
* **Net Revenue & Zero-Balance Accounts:** Because `monetary_value` nets positive sales (`+Quantity`) against cancellations/credits (`-Quantity`), **33 customer accounts** have `monetary_value = 0.00` and `frequency_count = 1` (representing orders fully reversed by a credit note or zero-priced entries).
* **Tableau Field Formatting:** When ingested into Tableau Desktop, `snake_case` headers automatically display in Title Case (e.g., `customer_id` $\rightarrow$ `Customer Id`, `monetary_value` $\rightarrow$ `Monetary Value`), and `customer_id` is cast to a discrete whole number dimension.
